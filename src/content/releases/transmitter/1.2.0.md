The alarms your CODESYS project already raises now show up in HotLoop's alarm list, and acknowledging one in HotLoop acknowledges it in the PLC. If you only use the publisher, there is nothing to change.

A real CODESYS Control for Linux SL 4.22.0.0 runtime ran it: a trip in the PLC shows in HotLoop, an acknowledge in HotLoop puts the PLC's alarm at acknowledged with the time, a reset waits for the confirm, and the confirm in HotLoop clears both. The publisher passed the full lab again, 128 checks across Variant and RawData, timestamps, open62541 and HotLoop's own subscriber, and the `.package` installs through the CODESYS Installer's command line.

What was not run: a physical controller, any runtime but Control for Linux SL 4.22.0.0, alarm classes other than the one that acknowledges and asks for a confirm, and more than 64 active alarms at once.

Download `HotLoopTransmitter-1.2.0.library`, the `.compiled-library` or the `.package`, and `HotLoopAlarms-1.0.0.0.library` or its `.compiled-library`, from [hotloop.io/hotloop-transmitter/1.2.0/](https://hotloop.io/hotloop-transmitter/1.2.0/HotLoopTransmitter-1.2.0.library), check them against `SHA256SUMS` in the same folder, and see the [install page](https://docs.hotloop.io/transmitter/install/).
