4.19.6 and 4.19.7 were tagged and never published. Each died in the Test job on a test that was telling the truth about a real bug, the second on a Logbook button that lost its page, and 4.19.8 carries everything both of their notes list, plus the fix. Nothing named 4.19.6 or 4.19.7 exists, and `stable` never moved to either.

This build is amd64 only, so it can reach production and read stable first. The next release is one small bump that builds the arm images, and `stable` keeps the arm images it holds, which are 4.19.5's.

One known gap: a Kafka or Redpanda history target whose broker refuses every write for lack of disk reports "records have timed out before they were able to be produced" and not why. The readings are not lost. The target keeps the batch, retries with a growing wait, and catches up from HotLoop's own history when the broker answers. If a Redpanda target is down with that message, look at the broker's log and its disk.
