4.23.6 brings the UniFi family into HotLoop, makes Modbus honest when a device goes quiet, and turns every finished feature on from the first start. It also carries everything 4.23.4 and 4.23.5 built, because neither was published.

## What changed

- **UniFi Protect and Access, as devices.** Cameras, sensors, lights and sirens, and each door's position and lock, are tags that add themselves. Every event is in the logbook, and an unlock is a write a person arms.
- **SmartPower PDU outlets.** An outlet's relay, power, current, voltage and power factor are tags, and writing an outlet's on switches it while keeping every other outlet's name.
- **A Shelly device.** Gen2 and later RPC with the device's own login, and writes for a switch, a light, a cover and a thermostat.
- **OPC UA PubSub security and metadata.** Aes128 and Aes256 signing and encryption, DataSetMetaData and discovery, chunks, structures and events.
- **A silent Modbus device reads bad on every point.** On a serial line the first request that timed out used the whole poll, so later points kept their last good value. Now every point goes bad in the same poll.
- **Finished features are on.** The broker, the OPC UA server with a generated read-only login, reports, the daily backup, alarm channels and the edge publisher are on from the first start. A channel with nowhere to send waits, and an admin gives it a destination under Settings, "Where alarms and readings go".
- **Carried from 4.23.4 and 4.23.5.** TwinCAT screens import with their buttons, frames and lamps, and the UniFi screen's refresh waits for the view that is loading.

## Upgrade

A plain `helm upgrade`. No migration and no new secret. This release builds the amd64 image, and the arm images follow in the next version.
