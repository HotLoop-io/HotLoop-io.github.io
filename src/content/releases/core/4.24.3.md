4.24.3 takes everything in 4.24.2 to every board we ship for, so the same release covers your servers, your Pis and your older ARM gateways. Read the 4.24.2 notes for everything new since 4.24.0.

## What changed

- **Every board.** The Gateway runs on amd64 and arm64. Edge, IoT and the Edge Relay run on amd64, arm64 and arm/v7.
- **Everything from 4.24.2.** Shelly of every generation, ONVIF cameras, Arduinos over Firmata, OPC UA passwords of any length, a forward queue that empties behind a slow link, a version command that names its commit, and a default HotLoop Edge install that starts.
- **The charts.** All four charts, `hotloop`, `hotloop-iot`, `hotloop-edge` and `hotloop-edge-relay`, are at 4.24.3, with every earlier version still in the index.

## Upgrade

Upgrade the way you always do: a `helm upgrade` to the 4.24.3 chart, or the new tag in your Quadlet unit. Nothing needs migrating.
