If your team does not read English at the screen, this is the release that lets them build and watch workflows in their own language. The choice is kept in the browser, and a first visit follows the browser's language.

## What changed

- **Every screen is translated:** workflows, runs, insights, variables, credentials, projects, users, roles, the audit log, settings, your account, secrets, log streaming and the step editor. Dates, numbers, durations and lists come from the browser's own formatting. Product names, node names, protocol names and example values stay in English in every language.
- **The words come from the shared HotLoop glossary.** A language is offered only when every line of it exists, and a test keeps the English step words and server messages equal to what the server can say, so a new step or refusal cannot ship without a line to translate.

## Upgrade

No migrations. `helm upgrade` to 1.3.0, or let `:stable` roll the Quadlet units. Putting 1.2.1 back is safe, because nothing in the database changed. This release builds amd64 only, so `stable`, `latest`, `1.3` and `1` list the new amd64 image beside the arm64 image they already pointed at.

## What isn't proven

- No native speaker has reviewed the translations yet.
- The update path was proven against a local registry standing in for ghcr.io, not ghcr.io itself.
- In the split install on PostgreSQL, the pods restart two or three times before the database's name resolves. They come up and the install passes. 1.2.1 does the same, and the fix is a connect retry in the next release.
