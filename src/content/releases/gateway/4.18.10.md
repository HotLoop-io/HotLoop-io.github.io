IoT, Edge, the Gateway and the Edge Relay all ship from this tag, in lockstep. 4.18.0 through 4.18.7 and 4.18.9 died in the Test job on real bugs, and 4.18.8 passed Test and was cancelled by mistake in the middle of publishing, so its four images were pushed as 4.18.8 and no chart and no release ever went out. 4.18.10 supersedes all of them. What isn't proven yet: the IoT and Edge Quadlet units under systemd, except IoT's SQLite unit, which was run, moving a site up a product as a helm upgrade on a real cluster, discovery against real devices on a bench, and the 32-bit arm images on real hardware.
