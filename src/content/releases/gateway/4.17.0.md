Coming from 4.16.0 it's a plain helm upgrade, and the notes above are the whole list of what bites. Migrations 0009 to 0015 run on the first start, all additive. Operators get helpers.write, scripts.run, recipes.apply and blueprints.apply the moment you upgrade; writing scripts, recipes and blueprints stays admin.
