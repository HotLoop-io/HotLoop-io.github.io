HotLoop Flow 2.14.0 lets an agent reach the rest of Flow over MCP, and drains a backlog after an outage in batches instead of one message at a time.

## What changed

- Flow's own MCP tools covered the flows and nothing around them. There are 22 more, 29 in all, and `docs/MCP.md` lists each with its route and permission. Sign-in, a person's own second factor and the live editor socket are not tools, and the page says why.
- A durable wire to a node that takes batches claims up to 256 messages at once. If a batch is not taken, the messages before the first one that failed are marked done, that one is retried alone, and the rest are sent again, so nothing is lost or reordered. Delivery is still at-least-once, and a message in flight when the process was killed can come again.
- The LICENSE file's Licensed Work line names only HotLoop Flow.

## Upgrade

A plain `helm upgrade` to the 2.14.0 image and chart, or the new tag in your Quadlet unit. Nothing happens on first start and nothing needs migrating. This release is amd64 only: arm64 boxes stay on 2.13.1 until 2.14.1.
