HotLoop Flow 2.8.1 is 2.8.0 with the arm64 image added, and nothing else. 2.8.0 shipped amd64 only so it could get to production and read stable first, and this is the one small bump that builds both. There is no change to the code, the chart, the settings or your flows.

## What changed

- The image is built for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64 again. The `2.8.1`, `2.8` and `2` tags hold both.
- The README and the install pages name 2.8.1 and say amd64 and arm64, as they did before 2.8.0.

## Upgrade

A plain `helm upgrade` to the 2.8.1 image and chart, or the new tag in your Quadlet unit. If you are on arm64 and stayed on 2.7.1 while 2.8.0 was amd64 only, this is your move to the analysis nodes, the data acquisition node, the durable MQTT output and the TDMS log: read the 2.8.0 notes for what they are. Nothing happens on first start and nothing needs migrating.
