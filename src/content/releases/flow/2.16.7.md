HotLoop Flow 2.16.7 puts the help for every node in all 14 languages the editor speaks. Nothing in your flows, settings or chart changes.

## What changed

- **Every node's help is translated.** The help text, field notes and the notes on how a node differs from Node-RED read in your language.
- **Plain wording from one shared glossary.** Each language uses the same terms the editor already used.

## Upgrade

A plain `helm upgrade` to the 2.16.7 image and chart, or the new tag in your Quadlet unit. Nothing needs migrating.
